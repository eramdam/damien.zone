What started as a crazy project back in 2023, and first saw the [light of day last year](https://damien.zone/introducing-mac-themes-garden), is finally complete!

> What do you mean by that? Wasn't it done when it launched already?

If only 😅! When I launched the website last year, I purposefully did it in a non-finished state. I knew if I waited to have all ~4,500 schemes to be completely catalogued, I would go slightly insane.   
Ever since I launched, I continued to hack away at the remaining themes, taking more and more screenshots from inside my OS 9 VM. 

But as of July 29th, I'm done![^1][^2]

## A bunch of funny numbers

I figured it would be fun to gather some statistics and numbers. Mac Themes Garden is, at time of writing this:

- 4,592 themes from 892 authors
- 13,801 screenshots
- [3,994 archives](https://files.macthemes.garden/)
- According to my very quick and dirty math, 60 consecutive days of work to take all the screenshots and record all the data by hand.
- ~65k total visits (a good 15% of that was from the first week post-launch thanks to whoever posted the announcement article to Hacker News)
- 4,170 followers for the bot account between [Mastodon](https://social.erambert.me/@macthemes/) and [Bluesky](https://bsky.app/profile/macthemes.garden/)

Here's the distribution of themes by year:

- **1997**: 1,103
- **1998**: 1,751
- **1999**: 921
- **2000**: 525
- **2001**: 196
- **2002**: 96

## What now?

I'm not sure! Initially I had wanted to look into extracting icons from the Kaleidoscope schemes and find a way to showcase them, but I haven't found a way to make it work in a satisfactory way. So I'm putting that on the backburner for now.

Something I *do* want to do and that, hopefully, wouldn't be too bad[^3] is find a way to serve https://macthemes.garden such that it can be loaded on Classic Macs. I will probably have to make a stripped down version of the site for this but we will see. 

I also started looking into how I could archive/record themes made for Mac OS X 10.4 using [ShapeShifter](https://macintoshgarden.org/apps/unsanity-shapeshifter). More on that later :\)
