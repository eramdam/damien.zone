In a very on-brand move, I saw Justice live in San Francisco last Thursday[^1], I took a bunch of pictures. BUT! This time, I used my newly-acquired Ricoh GR III, because what better way to really get familiarized with a camera than to use it to take hundreds of pictures for 3 hours in a dark environment, right?

Anyway, I got some pretty good ones, the Ricoh's resolution gives so much flexibility when cropping that the short focal length (28mm in 35mm equivalent) wasn't as big of an issue as I feared. And I wasn't even standing remotely close from the stage!

As always, click the images to see the full-size, full quality JPEGs. Only the third picture was taken with the iPhone 13 Pro using Halide, everything else was with the Ricoh.
